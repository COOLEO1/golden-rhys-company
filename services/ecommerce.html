Not necessarily. We can start with a catalogue and WhatsApp enquiries, or discuss online payments if a suitable provider is available.

Can I update products myself?

Product management can be included where the chosen solution supports it; we will agree the simplest workflow for your team.

How much does an online shop cost?

E-commerce scope varies considerably, so we prepare a quotation after discussing products, order handling, payment needs and management features.
