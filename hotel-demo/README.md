# Freddie's Lodge — Website Concept

A welcoming, lodge-style website concept prepared by Golden-Rhys Company for discussion with the property owner.

## Design direction
- Relaxed, modest lodge atmosphere rather than a large luxury hotel
- Warm natural colours, outdoor lodge imagery and simple room presentation
- Responsive desktop and mobile layout
- English and Chichewa language switch
- Sample room gallery, guest information and enquiry form

## Images and content
Stock imagery is sourced from Unsplash and Pexels and is used only to demonstrate the visual direction. Images do not depict confirmed Freddie's Lodge rooms or facilities. Room names, services, location details, prices, availability and policies must be replaced with owner-confirmed information before launch.

## Important
This is a demonstration concept, not the lodge's official website. The enquiry form is front-end only and does not send a real booking.
