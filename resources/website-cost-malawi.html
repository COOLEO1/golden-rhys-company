WEBSITE PLANNING · MALAWI

How much does a website cost in Malawi?

There is no single price for every website. The right budget depends on what your business needs the website to do, how much content it needs and which features are required.

What affects the price?

1. Number of pages and content

A focused one-page website is usually simpler than a larger site with separate pages for services, products, staff, locations and FAQs. Supplying clear text and good-quality images can also reduce preparation time.

2. Design and functionality

A straightforward business website may need a homepage, service information and contact options. An online catalogue, booking workflow, customer account or custom dashboard requires additional planning, development and testing.

3. Domain, hosting and third-party services

Ask whether the quotation includes the domain name, hosting, email, paid plugins, payment-provider charges and future renewals. These costs may be separate from the initial development fee.

4. Updates and support

Decide who will update photos, prices, products and announcements after launch. If you need ongoing technical support or regular content changes, agree how that work will be handled.

Common website starting points

A small business might begin with a single-page site that explains its offer and makes it easy to call or WhatsApp. A growing company may need several pages, a gallery and enquiry forms. A retailer may need a product catalogue or full online shop. The most useful choice is the smallest version that genuinely serves your customers and can be expanded later.

Questions to ask before accepting a quote

  • Which pages and features are included?
  • Will the website work well on mobile phones?
  • Who supplies the text, logo and photographs?
  • Are domain, hosting and renewals included?
  • Can I request changes and who maintains the site?
  • What is the delivery process and what happens before launch?
