Business website vs Facebook page: what does your business need?

Facebook can help people discover and follow a business. A website gives you a dedicated place to explain your services, organise information and guide customers towards an enquiry.

What a Facebook page does well

A Facebook page can be useful for regular updates, community conversations, comments, messages and sharing offers. It can help a business stay visible to people who already use the platform.

However, posts move down the feed, important details can be scattered across updates, and the page experience is controlled by the platform. A customer may have to search through posts to find your full service list, catalogue or policies.

What a business website adds

A website gives your business a dedicated online home under its own web address. You can organise information into clear pages, explain services in depth, show products, publish FAQs and make contact options easy to find.

  • Clear information: visitors can find services, opening details, location information and contact routes in one place.
  • Search visibility: useful pages can appear in search results when people look for relevant services.
  • More control: you choose the structure, content and customer journey.
  • Room to grow: a website can later add a catalogue, booking requests, forms or other features.

Do you have to choose one?

No. For many small businesses, the practical approach is to use both. Social media can help people discover your business and start conversations; your website can provide the complete, organised information and a clear next step.

A simple example

A furniture maker might share new work and short updates on Facebook. The website can organise furniture categories, show a gallery, explain custom-order options and provide a WhatsApp enquiry link. A lodge might use social posts for seasonal updates while its website explains rooms, facilities and booking requests.

When should you build a website?

Consider a website when customers repeatedly ask for the same information, when your services are difficult to explain in short posts, when you need a product or project gallery, or when you want a professional destination to share on business cards, proposals and social profiles.

Start with a useful first version

You do not need to build a large platform immediately. A focused website with a clear description of your business, key services, a few strong images and reliable contact options can be a sensible first step.
