THE TITHANDIZANE APPROACH

Good projects start with local understanding.

Tithandizane Construction & Civils is a fictional Malawi-focused construction brand. This concept shows how a contractor can present residential, commercial and renovation services, explain the project process and receive enquiries.

The experience is designed around what prospective clients need most: confidence in the process, visibility of previous work and a simple way to make an enquiry.

HOW WE WORK

A clear path from
plan to progress.

A straightforward process helps clients understand what happens next and feel informed throughout the journey.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand the brief

Discuss the location, plot or existing building, scope, budget range and timeline.

02 / PLAN

Shape the proposal

Prepare a scope of work and a written quotation in MWK, with materials and labour clearly explained.

03 / BUILD

Bring it to life

Coordinate agreed trades, materials and site progress with regular client updates.

04 / COMPLETE

Review & handover

Review the completed work, note any outstanding items and agree handover.
