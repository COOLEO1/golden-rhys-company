THE MWAYI DIFFERENCE

Confidence in every
step of the search.

Malawian buyers need useful details before travelling to view a vehicle. Make it easy to ask about mileage, service history, registration and viewing arrangements.

01 / DISCOVER

Easy vehicle browsing

Browse by body type, budget and intended use — from town commuting to family trips and work.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Useful information

Show mileage, engine, fuel, transmission, registration status, service history and clear photos.

03 / CONNECT

Direct enquiries

Send a WhatsApp enquiry, request a call or arrange an in-person viewing before making a decision.

YOUR NEXT DRIVE

From shortlist
to steering wheel.

A simple buyer journey helps visitors understand how to move from browsing a vehicle to making an enquiry.

01 / BROWSE

Explore the catalogue

02 / ENQUIRE

Ask your questions

Ask for the current MWK price, mileage, documents and vehicle condition.

03 / DISCUSS

Talk through options

Arrange a viewing in Lilongwe, Blantyre, Mzuzu or another agreed location.

04 / DECIDE

Make your next move

Inspect the vehicle and verify its documents before agreeing a purchase.
